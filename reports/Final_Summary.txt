----------------------------------------------------------
📄 ABSTRACT
----------------------------------------------------------
ClimateScope is a data-driven climate analysis application 
developed using Python and Streamlit. It analyzes environmental 
parameters such as temperature, humidity, and air quality.

The system includes a Climate Risk Alert feature that classifies 
risk levels into Low, Moderate, and High categories. The project 
provides interactive dashboards, comparisons, and visual insights 
to support climate understanding and decision-making.

----------------------------------------------------------
⚠️ CLIMATE RISK ALERT SYSTEM
----------------------------------------------------------
Risk Score Formula:
Risk = (0.5 × Temperature) + (0.3 × Humidity) + (0.2 × Air Quality)

Risk Levels:
- Low Risk
- Moderate Risk
- High Risk
